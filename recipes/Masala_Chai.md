# Masala chai

## Ingredients

### Initial boil

- 6 cups water
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp cloves
- 1/2 tsp black peppercorns
- 2 black cardamom
- 6 green cardamom pods
- 6 bay leaves
- 3 medium cinnamon sticks
- 4-inch piece of ginger root, peeled and chopped

### Final simmer

- 1/4 cup black tea
- 6 cups milk

## Directions

1. Boil water, spices, and sugar for 10 minutes
2. Add milk and tea, boil for 5 minutes
